# Soyuz

The original orbital Soyuz, 7K-OK, carried its pilot in the middle of three compartments. The forward compartment made room for living and working in orbit. The aft compartment carried power, propulsion and much of the equipment. Only the middle compartment came home. Active ships carried a docking probe, passive ships a socket. A crew changing ships had to go outside through the orbital compartment's side hatch.

## Gallery

[Soyuz 1](flights/soyuz-1/README.md) was the first crewed flight, after uncrewed tests. The gallery pairs spacecraft No. 4 with its original 11A511 launcher. Its hardware record describes the model from the aft skirt to the docking probe, and explains which features are specific to Komarov's flight. [Spacecraft record](flights/soyuz-1/spacecraft.md)

[Soyuz 3](flights/soyuz-3/README.md) brought a cosmonaut back to orbit after the loss of Soyuz 1.
Georgiy Beregovoy flew No. 10 from Site 31 and approached the uncrewed Soyuz 2. No. 10 returned
safely, and its surviving descent module is held by RKK Energia. The gallery shows both solar
wings open and reconstructs Beregovoy's couch and the orbital workroom.
