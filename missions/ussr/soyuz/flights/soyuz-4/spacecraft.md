# Soyuz 4 · 7K-OK(A) No. 12

No. 12 was the active ship in the Soyuz 4 and 5 pair. Its forward probe engaged the passive
cone on Soyuz 5, and two visitors returned in its descent capsule. The gallery uses the
[early 7K-OK envelope](../soyuz-1/spacecraft.md) and the
[early 11A511 stack](../../vehicles/soyuz-11a511.md).
[Shatalov's press conference, pp. 4–5](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19690007583/downloads/19690007583.pdf) ·
[Afanasyev and Vorontsov, *The First Soyuz Spacecraft*, Soyuz 4/5 chapter](https://epizodyspace.ru/bibl/mpk/mpk.pdf)

## What is simplified

- The gallery represents Soyuz 4 by itself. Soyuz 5, the transfer, the visitors' EVA suits
  and the visitors in their return couches are omitted. Their exact return-couch assignments
  remain unverified.
- The slider opens components for inspection, with illustrative array and antenna movement and
  separation distances. Rendezvous, docking and orbital and descent events are recorded in the
  timeline.
- The drawings establish a type baseline. Fabric and colour, hull stations, solar cells, panel
  legends and readings, hatch seals, and orbital-room fittings use estimates. The recovery
  hardware is represented externally, including the covers over the parachutes and landing motors.
