# Site 31, Tyuratam

Soyuz 3 left Site 31 on 26 October 1968, the first crewed launch from Tyuratam’s second Soyuz pad. The following January Vladimir Shatalov departed from it in Soyuz 4. The designation **Pad 6** came later. Vasily Mishin’s diary records the October rollout. The January launch photograph shows the upper portion of Soyuz against the sky and a slender lattice mast to one side. [Mishin, Diaries, vol. 2, pp. 105–111](https://techlibrary.ru/b1/2u1j1z1j1o_2j.2x._2l1o1f1c1o1j1l1j._2p1a1q1j1s1j_1j_1c1p1s1q1p1n1j1o1a1o1j2g_%281960-1974_1d1p1e2c%29._3a1p1n_2._2014.pdf) · [NASA, Challenge to Apollo, chapter 13](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/SP-4408pt2.pdf) · [Soyuz 4 launch photograph and pad history](https://www.russianspaceweb.com/baikonur_r7_31.html)

## Reconstruction limits

This model covers the deck, its approach, launch supports, open service platforms and excavation in October 1968 and January 1969. The exterior cladding, supporting columns, handrails, concrete joints, track termination and mast proportions are simplified. Evidence for those early launches does not establish the dates of changes to the work platforms. The individual umbilicals, underground rooms, transporter, assembly buildings, and service traffic remain unmodelled.
