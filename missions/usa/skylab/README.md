# Skylab

The workshop and its crews made separate launches. A two-stage Saturn V put the uncrewed Skylab 1 workshop in orbit on 14 May 1973. The three crews followed on Saturn IBs, using Apollo command and service modules equipped for weeks docked to the station. The crew patches numbered the visits from one, so the patch marked *Skylab I* belongs to the flight NASA designated Skylab 2. [NASA’s history of the first crew](https://www.nasa.gov/history/50-years-ago-skylab-2-the-we-fix-anything-crew/)

[Skylab 2](flights/skylab-2/README.md) brings CSM-116 and SA-206 to the Gallery and Lineup. Take a couch in the command module, find the workshop parasol packed below the seats, or open the service module to inspect its batteries and additional thruster propellant.

[Skylab 3](flights/skylab-3/README.md) adds CSM-117 and SA-207. Inspect the circadian experiment in the service module and the X-ray detector carried by the instrument unit. Compare the two Saturn IBs side by side in the Lineup, then switch to the spacecraft row to inspect CSM-117 on its own.
