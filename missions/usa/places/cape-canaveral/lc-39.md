# Launch Complex 39 in 1968–69

Apollo 8 and 9 stood at Pad A on mobile launchers 1 and 2. Apollo 10 stood at Pad B on mobile launcher 3. The model uses the common Saturn V launcher structure for all three. The tower is north of the launch axis. Pad A's open-air electrical substation stands west of the slope. Pad B housed its substation under the slope and had its water valve pit on the east side. Pad B's hardstand stood five feet higher above sea level. The Blender scripts use feet from the upper surface of the hardstand. The viewer uses metres from the vehicle axis at deck level. Each launch uses the civil time in force on its date. The Gallery strips the site from the stack.

## Reconstruction limits

The exhibit covers the launch hardstand, approach, launcher and principal storage spheres. The outer crawlerway network, mobile service structure in its remote parking area, VAB, underground rooms and most peripheral equipment are omitted. Launcher details common to the three units are assumed. Individual cable routes, internal machinery and foundations are simplified. The service-arm slider illustrates clearance and uses an estimated sweep. The site uses the Cape's default weather. Each crew member can be walked on the hardstand. Shared standing A7L figures represent the IV and EV launch garments. Body proportions, faces, small connectors and starting positions are illustrative. The access arm's final stair rise is reconstructed against the spacecraft hatch.
