# Saturn V SA-508 and adapter SLA-16

Apollo 13 flew S-IC-8, S-II-8, S-IVB-508 and IU-508 beneath SLA-16, LM-7 and CSM-109. The 363-foot stack weighed 6,501,733 lb at ground ignition and 6,414,967 lb at holddown-arm release according to the postflight reconstruction. The larger ignition mass of 6,505,746 lb in the mission plan was the prediction. Swigert docked Odyssey to Aquarius and extracted the lunar module after translunar injection. [AS-508 Flight Evaluation Report, p. xv and table 16-2](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/lvfea-AS508-Apollo13.pdf) · [Apollo 13 Press Kit, Saturn V launch vehicle](https://www.apollojournals.org/afj/ap13fj/pdf/a13-press-kit.pdf) · [Apollo 13 Mission Report, §3](https://www.apollojournals.org/afj/ap13fj/pdf/a13-mission-report-197007.pdf)

Four ullage-motor fairings stood around the aft end of the S-II interstage. S-II-8 was the first stage with sprayed foam insulation on the hydrogen tank sidewalls and forward skirt. During ascent, a longitudinal oscillation cut off its centre engine 132 seconds early. Its four outboard J-2 engines continued about 34 seconds beyond the predicted cutoff, and the S-IVB extended its first burn by about nine seconds. The Gallery presents the stage hardware in its launch configuration. [SA-508 Flight Manual, figs. 5-2 and 5-19](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/MSFC-MAN-508%20Saturn%20V%20Flight%20Manual%20SA-508%201-March-1970.pdf) · [AS-508 Flight Evaluation Report, §§3.5, 6.3, 7.1 and 8.2.3](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/lvfea-AS508-Apollo13.pdf)

## Tracking the spent stage

SA-508 carried a fourth 350 Ah silver-zinc battery to keep the command and communications system transmitting during the trip to the Moon. The flight manual depicts 6D20 across the bottom of cold plate 24. The CCS transponder, power amplifier and coaxial switch occupied the space above it. Engineers removed the isolators behind the plate to conduct heat into the instrument-unit structure and painted the cork on the outside white. After active cooling stopped, the battery acted as a heat sink for the amplifier. Tracking stations received the carrier until 77:56:41 ground elapsed time. Subtracting the signal’s travel time put the impact at 77:56:39.7, about 139 km from the Apollo 12 seismometer. [SA-508 Flight Manual, fig. 7-4 sheet 2, fig. 7-9 and §VII](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/MSFC-MAN-508%20Saturn%20V%20Flight%20Manual%20SA-508%201-March-1970.pdf) · [AS-508 Flight Evaluation Report, tables 4A-4, 4A-5, 11-4 and B-4](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/lvfea-AS508-Apollo13.pdf)

## Reconstruction limits

The common Saturn V structure supplies tank domes, engines, bearing lugs and stage stations. The adapter and stages separate along the Gallery’s inspection slider. The cold plate’s equipment placement follows the flight drawing. Case dimensions, surface finishes, terminal shapes and the painted cork area are estimated. The model omits the impact radio’s cabling, thermal shrouds, the texture of the S-II foam and most stage plumbing. The stage fairings’ contours and clocking use the adjacent flight reconstruction.
