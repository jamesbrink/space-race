# CSM-110 · Kitty Hawk

At launch Alan Shepard occupied the left couch, Stuart Roosa the centre and Edgar Mitchell the right. Roosa flew Kitty Hawk alone and photographed the Moon from orbit while Shepard and Mitchell explored Fra Mauro. [Apollo 14 Mission Report, MSC-04112, §§4 and 9](https://www.apollojournals.org/afj/ap14fj/pdf/a14_mission-report.pdf)

## Docking probe

After transposition the probe made six contacts with Antares. The crew counted the first two as one attempt because they had held forward thrust and brought the spacecraft back together. On the last contact they held forward thrust and commanded probe retraction until the docking rings latched. The crew returned the probe to Earth. Repeated testing failed to reproduce the problem. The Gallery displays the three capture hooks, retracting probe and twelve ring latches of the shared Block II mechanism. Its 10-inch stroke is **assumed** from the Block II operations handbook. [MSC-04112, §§9.5.1 and 14.1.1](https://www.apollojournals.org/afj/ap14fj/pdf/a14_mission-report.pdf) · [Apollo docking hardware](apollo-csm.md)

## Breathing oxygen and emergency power

The two original oxygen tank positions remained in sector IV above the hydrogen tanks and below the fuel cells. In sector I on the opposite side of the module, SM-110 carried a third tank. All three tanks could ordinarily supply both the fuel cells and the environmental control system. Closing the new isolation valve between tanks 2 and 3 reserved tank 3 for the environmental control system. The crew could then connect the auxiliary battery in place of fuel cell 2. The new controls for these actions were on panel 278. [MSC-04112, appendix A.1.3, fig. A-1 and appendix A.1.7](https://www.apollojournals.org/afj/ap14fj/pdf/a14_mission-report.pdf)

Each tank carried three heater elements. One had an independent control, allowing one, two or three elements to be powered. A temperature sensor monitored the heater. The quantity probe was stainless steel. The redesign removed the fan motors and heater thermal switches and moved the discharge filter into the external line. The reserve tank’s isolated instrument view shows the parallel heater and capacitance-probe columns suspended below the dome, using NASA’s drawing of the Apollo 14 tank. [MSC-04112, appendix A.1.3(a)](https://www.apollojournals.org/afj/ap14fj/pdf/a14_mission-report.pdf) · [NASA photograph S71-16745 reproduced on slide 37](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20080042304/downloads/20080042304.pdf)

The 400 Ah auxiliary battery on the sector IV aft bulkhead was a silver oxide–zinc LM descent battery. The flight press kit identifies it as identical to the four carried in the LM. Its modeled envelope of **16.94 × 9.04 × 9.96 inches** is **assumed** from the published descent battery nominal dimensions, excluding rails. A pair of motor switches could substitute this battery for fuel cell 2. A second control box protected the wiring from overload. [Apollo 14 Press Kit, pp. 96–97](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/apollo50th/pdf/A14_PressKit.pdf) · [NASA TN D-6976, table I and fig. 17](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19720024228/downloads/19720024228.pdf) · [MSC-04112, appendix A.1.3(e)](https://www.apollojournals.org/afj/ap14fj/pdf/a14_mission-report.pdf)

## Reconstruction limits

- The CSM service-engine exit to docking plane measures 404.25 inches in the common reconstruction. The pressure vessel, window outlines surveyed on Columbia, CM-106 hatch and console layout, couches, handheld drinking-water dispenser, high-gain antenna and service engine are **assumed** from sister spacecraft. The five one-gallon emergency water bags were stowed together in an aft-bulkhead locker and are omitted. Gauges are static. The flight’s additional panel 278 switches, deleted panel 2 oxygen fan controls and revised readouts are documented differences from the shared console. [Apollo CSM](apollo-csm.md) · [MSC-04112, appendix A.1](https://www.apollojournals.org/afj/ap14fj/pdf/a14_mission-report.pdf)
- The reserve dewar’s 26.55-inch outer diameter is **assumed** from the Block II oxygen tank. The exact tank station, three cradle legs and their web-to-web bearers, flange, outlet filter position and tubing bends are **estimated**. The cutaway omits the vacuum space, blankets, temperature sensors, insulation layers and long oxygen lines to the isolation valve. The probe and heater spacing, winding pitch and electrical conduits are **estimated** from S71-16745. The shared sector IV jackets show only their external envelope. [Common service tank dimensions](service-tanks.md)
- The battery’s local position, case finish, terminals, cover, rail and short cable geometry are **estimated** from the type envelope and the relative installation in figure 17. The exhibit omits the battery’s interior cells, control boxes and long harnesses. The docking animation depicts the normal mechanical sequence. It omits the unsuccessful contacts.
