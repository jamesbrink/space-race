# Columbia, command and service module 107

Armstrong flew from the left couch. For launch and translunar injection Aldrin took the centre couch and Collins the right. Collins moved to the left to turn Columbia, dock with Eagle and pull it clear of the Saturn. The three Gallery viewpoints use the launch arrangement. [Apollo 11 Flight Plan, crew positions](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/apollo50th/pdf/a11final-fltpln.pdf) · [Launch-day log](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/ap11ann/apollo11_log/log.htm)

The measured window openings, hatch interface, pressure-vessel openings and main-panel contour in the Block II builder come from this spacecraft and its detached door. The Smithsonian survey records Columbia after recovery. The launch exhibit restores the boost cover, forward shield, packed parachutes, service module and probe. The heat shield carries its pre-entry finish. The three couches, sextant and scanning telescope use the common Block II installation. Flight image AS11-36-5388 shows Armstrong beside the lower navigation station during the outward voyage. [Survey registration and dimensions](reference/README.md) · [Smithsonian flight hatch](https://www.si.edu/object/hatch-crew-apollo-11%3Anasm_A19791810000) · [Flight image library](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/a11/images11.html#5388)

In the left forward bay, the dispenser carried a six-inch cartridge. The fuel cells made water with gas still dissolved in it. Gas and water passed through different membranes in the cartridge. The crew demonstrated the dispenser on the way home. Cabinet position within the bay, handpiece, clips, nozzle and cartridge diameter are estimated. ## Reconstruction limits

The CM-107 console outline and instrument interfaces are survey-based. Individual controls use the CM-106 drawing as a documented sister-ship assumption. The lightweight hatch's pocket envelopes come from CM-106. The DSKY housing and inner display use earlier equipment references. Apollo 11 carried assembly 2003994-121. Lens and finish differences are unresolved. Switch settings, lighting, eye positions and soft equipment are illustrative. The new forward-hatch insulation, entry battery separators, modified battery chargers and high-gain antenna servo are recorded configuration changes whose internal construction is omitted.
