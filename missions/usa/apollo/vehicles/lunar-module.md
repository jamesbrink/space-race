# LM-3 Spider

The drawing datum is the plane of the deployed footpads. Blender uses inches and converts to metres once. The four surface probes extend below that plane. In the standalone Gallery exhibit the spacecraft has extended gear and antenna. The launch stack builds the same parts with folded gear and a stowed antenna.

The height ends at the S-band antenna, not at the docking ring. The ascent and descent stage structures, gear and system counts come from the flown vehicle report. The press kit describes the two pilots standing at their controls. Photographs constrain the forward window shapes, black and metallic insulation, copper-gold descent blankets, ladder, porch and foot restraints. [Mission Report, appendix A.2 and figs. The descent stage contains the cruciform structure, throttleable engine, four propellant tanks, pressurization vessels, four 400 Ah batteries and one water and one oxygen vessel. The landing radar looks down from the underside. The ascent stage retains its fixed engine, two propellant tanks, two 310 Ah batteries, two oxygen and two water tanks, suit-circuit hardware, two reaction-control supplies and four four-nozzle clusters. Inside the pressure cabin the two stations have armrests and restraints, side breaker panels, a central display and keyboard, an alignment optical telescope, barrel frames and handholds over the ascent-engine cover. The side instrument banks and optical alignment are estimated from the operations handbook. Engine views isolate the chamber, jacket, nozzle extension and mounts. The nominal DPS thrust is 9,870 lbf. The mission report gives 10,500 lbf as the upper operating figure. The APS was rated at 3,500 lbf. [Mission Report, A.2.4, A.2.7–13, figs. A.2-16 through A.2-24](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/A09_MissionReport.pdf) · [NASA spacecraft engine dimensions, table I](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19700026405/downloads/19700026405.pdf)

## Reconstruction limits

The flight drawing controls the major envelopes. Panel boundaries, foil folds, local tank and equipment positions, pipe and harness paths, brackets, instrument faces, restraints and the eye positions are estimates. The alignment telescope is shown from the crew side. Its exterior optical head and light path through the roof are omitted. The equipment schematics establish counts and connections but do not dimension every installation. The model depicts the principal assemblies. It omits individual electrical circuits, complete environmental plumbing, deployable personal equipment, and detailed internal engine mechanisms. The illustrative gear, antenna and staging motions have estimated angles and timings. The two station availability intervals approximate the span between each astronaut’s first ingress and final transfer in the mission report’s activity chart, including periods spent elsewhere. The Gallery does not simulate occupancy. [Apollo Operations Handbook, LM](https://web.mit.edu/digitalapollo/Documents/Chapter8/lemhandbook.pdf)
