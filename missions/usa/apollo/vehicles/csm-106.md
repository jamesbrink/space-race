# Charlie Brown’s hatch and main console

The command module on Apollo 10 introduced a lighter crew hatch. North American machined pockets into its inner aluminum slab, leaving 0.10 inch of metal at the bottom of each pocket. A ratchet handle on the counterbalance made it easier to operate by hand. The Smithsonian holds the flown hatch with its window and latch hardware removed. Its collection photograph shows the recesses around the window, between the latch stations and across the lower part of the door. [NASA TN D-7780, printed p. 12](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19740026226/downloads/19740026226.pdf) · [Apollo 10 Mission Report, §A.1.1](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/A10_MissionReport.pdf) · [Smithsonian, Apollo 10 hatch, NASM-A19781415000cp01](https://ids.si.edu/ids/deliveryService?id=NASM-A19781415000cp01)

Sixteen visible pocket outlines are reconstructed in the inner face of the modeled hatch. Their positions come from the museum photograph, projected from four perimeter corners into the shared hatch plane. The pane opening and latch ring keep their independent Block II references. The reconstruction estimates the depth from the modeled slab thickness and NASA’s remaining wall thickness. The flight door in the Gallery retains its window and latch hardware.

North American Rockwell drawing V36-976024 identifies the arrangements of panels 1, 2 and 3 in CM-106. Registering the sheet against the earlier CM-104 drawing gives 1,686 matching printed features across the panel. Two attitude-indicator centres position the sheet on the shared structural survey. The resulting keyboard centre differs from the DSKY equipment reference by 0.78 cm. The CM-106 reference JSON records the source hash, crop, registration and independent check. It reuses the overlapping controls and replaces the radio and telemetry rows on panel 3. Two thumbwheels set channel A and B squelch. The main panel's entry monitor could display distance to Snoopy using the new VHF ranging equipment. [V36-976024, sheet 1](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/V36-976024_Design_Layout_MDC_CM-106.pdf) · [Mission Report, §§A.1.2 and A.1.4](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/A10_MissionReport.pdf)

## Reconstruction limits

The hatch pocket positions, local corner rounding and depths are estimates. Small or obscured recesses, fastener holes and the internal counterbalance are omitted. The model uses the shared latch linkage. The window, inner door pose and console structure are registered from other Block II artifacts. Panel placements use a design drawing. Toggle profiles, thumbwheel relief, legends and other small controls are simplified, the side consoles are incomplete, and the instruments show a static state.
