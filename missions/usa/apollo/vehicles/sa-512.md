# SA-512 · Saturn V and SLA-21

America and Challenger left LC-39A on 7 December 1972 aboard S-IC-12, S-II-12, S-IVB-512, IU-512 and adapter SLA-21. The nominal assembled height was 363 ft. The reconstructed masses were **2,961,860 kg at ground ignition** and **2,917,844 kg at hold-down-arm release**. These are separate events while the engines were consuming propellant. [AS-512 Flight Evaluation Report, launch vehicle description and table 16-1](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19730025089) · [Apollo 17 Press Kit, countdown and pp. 93–96](https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Apollo_17.pdf)

## Stage separation and the trip to the Moon

Two forward-facing retro motors under each fin fairing pulled the first stage away from the second. Each of the four pairs can be inspected separately. The second stage had five J-2 engines and a centre-engine pogo accumulator. The third-stage J-2 fired for 138.8 seconds for parking orbit and 351.0 seconds for translunar injection. The four adapter panels then swung out of the way, letting Evans turn America and dock it with Challenger. [AS-512 Flight Evaluation Report, §§6, 7 and 10](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19730025089) · [Apollo 17 Mission Report, table 3-I](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19730015117)

Two auxiliary propulsion modules mounted on opposite sides of the S-IVB each held three attitude engines and an aft-facing ullage engine. After spacecraft extraction, controllers fired the ullage engines for 98 seconds at 6 h 10 min elapsed and 102 seconds at 11 h 15 min. The IU tracking radio allowed controllers to follow the spent stage on its way to the Moon. Flight tracking placed the impact at 4.33° S, 12.37° W, 155 km from the intended target, at 2,544 m/s. The 2016 LROC survey located the crater at **4.1681° S, 12.3307° W**. [AS-512 Flight Evaluation Report, §§7 and 17](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19730025089) · [LROC Coordinates of Lunar Anthropogenic Targets, 28 July 2016](https://www.lroc.asu.edu/data/support/downloads/2016_LROC_Coordinates_of_Robotic_Spacecraft.pdf)

## Reconstruction limits

Stage shells, J-2 and F-1 contours, propellant tanks, markings, adapter hinges and the pogo accumulator use the [shared Saturn V geometry](saturn-v.md). Internal retro case proportions and mounting hardware are assumed from [SA-510](sa-510.md), with eight motors documented for SA-512. The APS pods use common S-IVB drawings. Local feed lines, restraints, IU battery cases and their placement are estimated. The Gallery slider separates the stages for inspection and compresses the adapter-panel motion into a single stroke.
