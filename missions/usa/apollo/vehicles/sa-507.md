# Saturn V SA-507 and adapter SLA-15

Apollo 12 flew S-IC-7, S-II-7, S-IVB-7N and instrument unit IU-7. SLA-15 enclosed Intrepid under the service propulsion engine. The launch vehicle weighed 6,484,620 lb at ground ignition. The press kit gives the assembled height as 363 ft. [AS-507 Flight Evaluation Report, §2, p. 2-1](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/lvfea-AS507-Apollo12.pdf) · [Apollo 12 Press Kit, Saturn V Launch Vehicle](https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Apollo_12.pdf)

The S-IC carried five F-1 engines, S-II five J-2s, and the restartable S-IVB a single J-2. Four ullage assemblies stood at quarter turns around the aft end of the cylindrical S-IC/S-II interstage. They travelled with that interstage when it fell away. The S-IC of SA-507 omitted the offset Doppler tracking system. The S-IVB carried an additional telemetry link relative to SA-506. The manual records no major changes between their S-II stages or instrument units. [SA-507 Flight Manual, §§IV–VII, figs. 5-2 and 5-19 and “Major Differences”](https://www.apollojournals.org/afj/ap12fj/pdf/a12_sa507-flightmanual.pdf)

The four adapter panels opened for transposition and separated. Gordon turned Yankee Clipper around, docked with Intrepid and drew it out. The mission report identifies the adapter configuration as identical to Apollo 11’s. [Apollo 12 Mission Report, §§1 and A.1](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/a12-missionreport.pdf)

## Reconstruction limits

The common Saturn V reconstruction supplies the stage stations, engine and tank profiles, instrument-ring case envelopes, bearing lugs and paint patterns. These are assumed for SA-507 wherever the flight manual lacks an installation dimension. The interstage fairing profiles and clocking are estimated. The stage cutaways omit individual cable runs, most plumbing and detailed telemetry equipment. The Gallery slider separates the hardware for inspection.
