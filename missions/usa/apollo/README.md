# Apollo

Apollo 7 is this programme’s first model: CSM-101, a Block II command and service module,
launched on Saturn IB SA-205 on 11 October 1968. Apollo 1 did not fly; Apollo 7 was the first
crewed Apollo flight. The programme’s lunar spacecraft must be their own dated configurations,
not copies of this Earth-orbit test vehicle.

Shared hardware lives in `vehicles/`; a flight’s model script selects its configuration.
Apollo 7 is in the gallery: the whole stack, cut open to its stages, its service module and its cabin, which can be
sat in. Its launch site and the flight itself are not built.

[Apollo 8](flights/apollo-8/README.md) adds CSM-103 on Saturn V SA-503, with the four-dish lunar antenna and the instrumented test article inside the adapter. Its Gallery includes all three crew seats.

[Apollo 9](flights/apollo-9/README.md) adds CSM-104 Gumdrop and the first crewed lunar module, LM-3 Spider. The Gallery shows both the launch installation on SA-504 and Spider deployed as a separate spacecraft.

[Apollo 10](flights/apollo-10/README.md) pairs Charlie Brown (CSM-106) with Snoopy (LM-4) on SA-505. Snoopy also stands deployed, with the standing stations and the ascent stage that returned to lunar orbit rendezvous.

[Apollo 11](flights/apollo-11/README.md) adds Columbia (CSM-107), Eagle (LM-5) and SA-506. Explore the folded LM aboard the Saturn V or the deployed Eagle with its landing equipment and two standing stations.
