# CSM-104 Gumdrop on SA-504

Gumdrop carried the active probe for docking with LM-3. After capture, the probe retracted until the rings met. The crew removed tunnel hardware to pass between the ships. Twelve docking latches held the structural joint. A target inside the right rendezvous window gave Spider’s crew an alignment reference. The mission report describes a green electroluminescent base and a projecting red target. Their dimensions and the backplate markings are estimated against the surveyed glazing. [Apollo Program Summary Report, section 4.7.5](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19750013242/downloads/19750013242.pdf)

The high-gain antenna stayed with the service module. [Apollo 9 Mission Report, appendix A.1](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/A09_MissionReport.pdf) · [NASA TN D-6854, docking system](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19720018207/downloads/19720018207.pdf)

The command and service modules inherit the Block II shell, hatch, windows, seats, service tanks and engine reconstructed for Apollo 7 and 8. The main console uses North American drawing **V36-976021** for CM-104. Two flight-director centres register the drawing to the shared structural survey of CM-107. The independent DSKY check differs by 0.72 cm. The controls are static. Switch relief, secondary legends and equipment depths are estimated. [Main display console design layout, CM-104](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/V36-976021_Design_Layout_MDC_CM-104.pdf)

At transposition the CSM weighed 59,085 lb. The launch/ignition figure is 59,116 lb. The LM weighed 32,132 lb at launch and its ascent stage 10,216 lb at staging. The distinct event and mass definitions are retained in the flight data. [Mission Report, table A.5-I](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/A09_MissionReport.pdf)

SA-504 used the shared Saturn V thrust-post reconstruction, including the exposed F-1 cluster and the 182 in outboard mounting radius assumed from SA-503. Adapter SLA-13A held a flight lunar module. The split is 85.9 in above its base, derived from XA 587.9 minus XA 502. The updated S-II ratings and launch mass are recorded in the flight data. [AS-504 technical summary, figs. 3, 33, 42 and 47](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19700009516/downloads/19700009516.pdf) · [Saturn V construction and limits](../../vehicles/saturn-v.md)

## Reconstruction limits

The probe stroke and individual fittings, cabin lighting, seat-eye positions, pad boarding interval, inspection motions, external cable routing and most small service-module fittings are estimates. The instrument-unit equipment bays retain the SA-503 reconstruction. Supplemental exterior lights, film cameras, functional instruments and a simulated docking sequence are omitted. LM-3 has its own hardware notes and simplifications. [LM-3 Spider](../../vehicles/lunar-module.md)
