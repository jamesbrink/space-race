# CSM-104 Gumdrop on SA-504

Gumdrop carried the active probe for docking with LM-3. Three capture latches in the head engaged the drogue socket. Probe retraction drew the rings together. Twelve peripheral latches held the structural joint against two concentric seals. With the joint secure, the crew removed the probe and drogue to open the transfer tunnel. The model places the mating frame at command-module station 110.25 in, giving 404.25 in from the service-engine exit to the docking plane. [NASA TN D-6854, figs. 9, A-5 and A-13](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19720018207/downloads/19720018207.pdf)

A target inside the right rendezvous window gave Spider’s crew an alignment reference. The mission report describes a green electroluminescent base and a projecting red target. Their dimensions and the backplate markings are estimated against the surveyed glazing. [Apollo Program Summary Report, section 4.7.5](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19750013242/downloads/19750013242.pdf)

In the Gallery the probe extends 10 inches (0.254 m), the maximum stroke specified for the Block II inner cylinder. At aligned capture, the ring planes stand that distance apart. At hard dock, they meet. [Apollo Operations Handbook, vol. 1, §2.13.3.3.4, p. 2.13-17](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/ApolloProjectOnline/Documents/SMA2A-03-BLOCK%20II%20Volume%201%2019691015/aoh-v1-2-13-dock.pdf)

The high-gain antenna stayed with the service module. [Apollo 9 Mission Report, appendix A.1](https://coastalreview.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Apollo-9-mission-report.pdf) · [NASA TN D-6854, docking system](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19720018207/downloads/19720018207.pdf)

The command and service modules inherit the Block II shell, hatch, windows, seats, service tanks and engine reconstructed for Apollo 7 and 8. The main console uses North American drawing **V36-976021** for CM-104. Two flight-director centres register the drawing to the shared structural survey of CM-107. The independent DSKY check differs by 0.72 cm. The controls are static. Switch relief, secondary legends and equipment depths are estimated. [Main display console design layout, CM-104](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/V36-976021_Design_Layout_MDC_CM-104.pdf)

At transposition the CSM weighed 59,085 lb. The LM weighed 32,132 lb at launch and its ascent stage 10,216 lb at staging. SP-4029's ground ignition weights give the CSM as 59,116 lb, a different event and definition. The flight data keeps each under its own label. [Mission Report, table A.5-I](https://coastalreview.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Apollo-9-mission-report.pdf)

SA-504 used the shared Saturn V thrust-post reconstruction, including the exposed F-1 cluster and the 182 in outboard mounting radius assumed from SA-503. Adapter SLA-13A held a flight lunar module. The split is 85.9 in above its base, derived from XA 587.9 minus XA 502. The updated S-II ratings and launch mass are recorded in the flight data. [AS-504 technical summary, figs. 3, 33, 42 and 47](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19700009516/downloads/19700009516.pdf) · [Saturn V construction and limits](../../vehicles/saturn-v.md)

## Reconstruction limits

The three capture hooks have an estimated 0.34-inch radial travel, and the twelve ring latches have an estimated 24° closing sweep. Individual probe fittings, cabin lighting, seat-eye positions, pad boarding interval, inspection motions, external cable routing and most small service-module fittings are estimates. The instrument-unit equipment bays retain the SA-503 reconstruction. Supplemental exterior lights, film cameras, functional instruments and a simulated docking sequence are omitted. LM-3 has its own hardware notes and simplifications. [LM-3 Spider](../../vehicles/lunar-module.md)
