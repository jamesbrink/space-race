# Project Gemini (USA, 1961–1966)

NASA's second human spaceflight programme, between Mercury and Apollo: two men in a ship that could
change its orbit, stay up for two weeks, find another vehicle and dock with it, and be flown down
to a chosen place. Ten crewed flights in twenty months, all in the two-man spacecraft McDonnell
Aircraft built in St. Louis, all on Martin's Titan II from Launch Complex 19.

Two unmanned flights came first: Gemini-Titan 1 (8 April 1964, into orbit, not recovered) and
Gemini-Titan 2 (19 January 1965, a ballistic test of the heat shield). Gemini-Titan 3 to 7 have
been researched beyond that table, each from its own mission report where one was found (Gemini
VI-A's was not); what is said below of the ships after spacecraft 7 is to be checked when each is
built.

## The spacecraft, in general

A re-entry module 11 ft long, shaped like a Mercury capsule with half as much room again inside,
on a white adapter that widens to the Titan's 10 ft. The crew sit side by side under two hatches, on ejection
seats: there is no escape tower. Everything needed only in orbit is in the adapter, which is thrown
away in two pieces round the retrofire: [gemini-spacecraft.md](vehicles/gemini-spacecraft.md).

The ship changed from flight to flight, which is why each flight here will have its own model:

| | No. 3 | No. 4 | No. 5 | No. 6 | No. 7 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Power | 3 batteries of 400 Ah in the adapter | 6 | two fuel cell sections | 3 batteries | two fuel cell sections |
| Rendezvous radar | none | none | fitted, with a pod to try it on | fitted, and used | none: a transponder to answer No. 6's |
| For docking | nothing | nothing | nothing | three latch receptacles and an index bar, for an Agena that was lost (that they flew is assumed) | nothing |
| Sideways thrusters | dummies | real | real | real | real |
| OAMS tanks | one of fuel, one of oxidiser, one of helium | the same | the same | two of each | one of each, and a reserve tank of fuel |
| Drinking water in the adapter | 1 tank | 4 | 2 larger, one taking the fuel cells' water | 1 | those 2 and a third in the retrograde section |
| To be found by | | | | | two flashing lights at the adapter's foot |
| Going outside | no provision | an umbilical and a hand-held gas gun | none | none | none |
| Suit | G3C | G4C | G4C | G4C | G5C, which could be taken off |

All documented, each in its flight's own record: [No. 3](flights/gt-3/spacecraft.md),
[No. 4](flights/gt-4/spacecraft.md), [No. 5](flights/gt-5/spacecraft.md),
[No. 6](flights/gt-6a/spacecraft.md), [No. 7](flights/gt-7/spacecraft.md). Spacecraft 7 flew before
spacecraft 6. The spacecraft's module
takes each difference as an argument ([gemini-spacecraft.md](vehicles/gemini-spacecraft.md), "One
