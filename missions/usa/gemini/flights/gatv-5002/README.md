# GATV-5002

The target was lost during ascent on 25 October 1965. Schirra and Stafford were still on the pad. They flew in December to meet Gemini VII instead.

Atlas SLV-3 5301 launched this uncrewed target from Cape Kennedy Launch Complex 14 on 1965-10-25 at 15:00:04.490 UTC. It carried docking adapter 2. The gallery shows the target separately at scale. The launcher and its dated pad are still absent. The exterior is shown with the launch shroud removed to expose the docking equipment. This is an exhibit configuration for the lost vehicle.

Sources include NASA SP-4002, Appendix I, Table B, and NASA CR-1106, pp. 334–336. See the [hardware notes](../../vehicles/agena-target-vehicle.md) for the geometry and its confidence.
