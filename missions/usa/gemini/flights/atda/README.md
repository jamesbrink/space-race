# ATDA

Launched as the replacement for GATV-5004, the shorter target kept both halves of its shroud. Stafford and Cernan flew three rendezvous. The obstructed cone prevented docking.

Atlas SLV-3 5304 launched this uncrewed target from Cape Kennedy Launch Complex 14 on 1966-06-01 at 15:00:02.363 UTC. It carried docking adapter 4. The gallery shows the target separately at scale. The [assembled Atlas](../../vehicles/atlas-slv3.md) stands at the dated launch site with its shroud closed. The shroud is shown as encountered in orbit.

Sources include NASA SP-4002, Appendix I, Table B, and NASA CR-1106, pp. 334–336. See the [hardware notes](../../vehicles/atda.md) for the geometry and its confidence.
