# ATDA

Launched as the replacement for GATV-5004, the shorter target kept both halves of its shroud. Stafford and Cernan flew three rendezvous. The obstructed cone prevented docking.

Atlas SLV-3 5304 launched this uncrewed target from Cape Kennedy Launch Complex 14 on 1966-06-01 at 15:00:02.363 UTC. It carried docking adapter 4. The gallery shows the target separately at scale. The [assembled Atlas](../../vehicles/atlas-slv3.md) stands at the dated launch site with its shroud closed. The shroud is shown as encountered in orbit.

Sources include NASA SP-4002, Appendix I, Table B, and NASA CR-1106, pp. 334–336. See the [hardware notes](../../vehicles/atda.md) for the geometry and its confidence.

## Flown

Fly flies the launch from liftoff, from the Gemini IX-A report’s figures: the Atlas put the ATDA in orbit itself, its sustainer cut by the guidance at the planned speed and its verniers burning on alone to 367.5 s, and the ATDA let go at 383.4 s with its shroud on. The run reaches 161.2 by 176.3 nautical miles; the record has 161.0 by 167.1 (the verniers add too much: misses.ts). The shroud stays on, as it did. The Atlas is the Mercury-Atlas flights’ (the same plan pieces), its weights this launch’s report’s and its thrusts Atlas 5302’s; its steering is a stated law solved for the target’s insertion, since the Atlas’s programme and radio guidance were not found. Every input that is not documented, and every check missed, is listed in [orbital.ts](orbital.ts), [target-plan.ts](../../vehicles/target-plan.ts) and src/sim/misses.ts.
