# GATV-5001

Lovell and Aldrin docked with the refurbished target after their rendezvous radar failed. The fittings on its adapter gave Aldrin secure holds while working outside.

Atlas SLV-3 5307 launched this uncrewed target from Cape Kennedy Launch Complex 14 on 1966-11-11 at 19:07:58.688 UTC. It carried docking adapter 7A. The gallery shows the target separately at scale. The launcher and its dated pad are still absent. The exterior is shown with the launch shroud removed to expose the docking equipment.

Sources include NASA SP-4002, Appendix I, Table B, and NASA CR-1106, pp. 334–336. See the [hardware notes](../../vehicles/agena-target-vehicle.md) for the geometry and its confidence.
