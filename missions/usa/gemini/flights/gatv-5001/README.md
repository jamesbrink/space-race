# GATV-5001

Lovell and Aldrin docked with the refurbished target after their rendezvous radar failed. The fittings on its adapter gave Aldrin secure holds while working outside.

Atlas SLV-3 5307 launched this uncrewed target from Cape Kennedy Launch Complex 14 on 1966-11-11 at 19:07:58.688 UTC. It carried docking adapter 7A. The gallery shows the target separately at scale. The [assembled Atlas](../../vehicles/atlas-slv3.md) stands at the dated launch site with its shroud closed. The exterior is shown with the launch shroud removed to expose the docking equipment.

Sources include NASA SP-4002, Appendix I, Table B, and NASA CR-1106, pp. 334–336. See the [hardware notes](../../vehicles/agena-target-vehicle.md) for the geometry and its confidence.

## Flown

Fly flies the launch from liftoff, from the Gemini XII report’s figures. The Agena is cut at the planned speed at 556.0 s (record 555.9), for 160.6 by 168.4 nautical miles against the record’s 159.0 by 163.6. The momentary thrust decay of its ascent burn is not flown. It stays up. The Atlas is the Mercury-Atlas flights’ (the same plan pieces), its weights this launch’s report’s and its thrusts Atlas 5302’s; its steering is a stated law solved for the target’s insertion, since the Atlas’s programme and radio guidance were not found. Every input that is not documented, and every check missed, is listed in [orbital.ts](orbital.ts), [target-plan.ts](../../vehicles/target-plan.ts) and src/sim/misses.ts.
