# GATV-5006

Conrad and Gordon docked on their first orbit. The Agena later raised the joined spacecraft to a high apogee. Gordon attached the tether for the subsequent separation experiment.

Atlas SLV-3 5306 launched this uncrewed target from Cape Kennedy Launch Complex 14 on 1966-09-12 at 13:05:01.725 UTC. It carried docking adapter 6. The gallery shows the target separately at scale. The [assembled Atlas](../../vehicles/atlas-slv3.md) stands at the dated launch site with its shroud closed. The exterior is shown with the launch shroud removed to expose the docking equipment.

Sources include NASA SP-4002, Appendix I, Table B, and NASA CR-1106, pp. 334–336. See the [hardware notes](../../vehicles/agena-target-vehicle.md) for the geometry and its confidence.

## Flown

Fly flies the launch from liftoff, from the Gemini XI report’s figures. The Agena is cut at the planned speed at 556.3 s (record 558.4), for 159.7 by 170.9 nautical miles against the record’s 156.0 by 165.4. It stays up. The Atlas is the Mercury-Atlas flights’ (the same plan pieces), its weights this launch’s report’s and its thrusts Atlas 5302’s; its steering is a stated law solved for the target’s insertion, since the Atlas’s programme and radio guidance were not found. Every input that is not documented, and every check missed, is listed in [orbital.ts](orbital.ts), [target-plan.ts](../../vehicles/target-plan.ts) and src/sim/misses.ts.
