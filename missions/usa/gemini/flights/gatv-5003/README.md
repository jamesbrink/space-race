# GATV-5003

Armstrong and Scott docked with this Agena in March. Four months later Collins reached it again from Gemini X and retrieved its micrometeorite collector.

Atlas SLV-3 5302 launched this uncrewed target from Cape Kennedy Launch Complex 14 on 1966-03-16 at 15:00:03.127 UTC. It carried docking adapter 3. The gallery shows the target separately at scale. The [assembled Atlas](../../vehicles/atlas-slv3.md) stands at the dated launch site with its shroud closed. The exterior is shown with the launch shroud removed to expose the docking equipment.

Sources include NASA SP-4002, Appendix I, Table B, and NASA CR-1106, pp. 334–336. See the [hardware notes](../../vehicles/agena-target-vehicle.md) for the geometry and its confidence.

## Flown

Fly flies the launch from liftoff, from the Gemini VIII report’s weights, sequence and trajectory: the Atlas to vernier cutoff, the Agena let go at 308.3 s, its minute’s coast, and its engine from 377.5 s, cut at the planned insertion speed. The run reaches 161.3 by 162.7 nautical miles at 561.0 s; the record has 161.4 by 165.6 at 560.4 s. In orbit the Agena is flown round for six hours and stays up. The Atlas is the Mercury-Atlas flights’ (the same plan pieces), its weights this launch’s report’s and its thrusts Atlas 5302’s; its steering is a stated law solved for the target’s insertion, since the Atlas’s programme and radio guidance were not found. Every input that is not documented, and every check missed, is listed in [orbital.ts](orbital.ts), [target-plan.ts](../../vehicles/target-plan.ts) and src/sim/misses.ts.
