# GATV-5005

Young and Collins docked with their Agena and used its engine to change orbit. They then left it to visit the dormant target from Gemini VIII.

Atlas SLV-3 5305 launched this uncrewed target from Cape Kennedy Launch Complex 14 on 1966-07-18 at 20:39:46.131 UTC. It carried docking adapter 1A. The gallery shows the target separately at scale. The [assembled Atlas](../../vehicles/atlas-slv3.md) stands at the dated launch site with its shroud closed. The exterior is shown with the launch shroud removed to expose the docking equipment.

Sources include NASA SP-4002, Appendix I, Table B, and NASA CR-1106, pp. 334–336. See the [hardware notes](../../vehicles/agena-target-vehicle.md) for the geometry and its confidence.

## Flown

Fly flies the launch from liftoff, from the Gemini X report’s figures. The Agena’s engine reaches the planned insertion speed at 553.6 s, 4.5 s before the record’s cutoff, for 160.6 by 167.4 nautical miles against the record’s 158.5 by 164.3. It stays up. The Atlas is the Mercury-Atlas flights’ (the same plan pieces), its weights this launch’s report’s and its thrusts Atlas 5302’s; its steering is a stated law solved for the target’s insertion, since the Atlas’s programme and radio guidance were not found. Every input that is not documented, and every check missed, is listed in [orbital.ts](orbital.ts), [target-plan.ts](../../vehicles/target-plan.ts) and src/sim/misses.ts.
