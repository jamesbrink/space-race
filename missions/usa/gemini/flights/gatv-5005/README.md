# GATV-5005

Young and Collins docked with their Agena and used its engine to change orbit. They then left it to visit the dormant target from Gemini VIII.

Atlas SLV-3 5305 launched this uncrewed target from Cape Kennedy Launch Complex 14 on 1966-07-18 at 20:39:46.131 UTC. It carried docking adapter 1A. The gallery shows the target separately at scale. The launcher and its dated pad are still absent. The exterior is shown with the launch shroud removed to expose the docking equipment.

Sources include NASA SP-4002, Appendix I, Table B, and NASA CR-1106, pp. 334–336. See the [hardware notes](../../vehicles/agena-target-vehicle.md) for the geometry and its confidence.
