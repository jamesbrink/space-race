# Gemini spacecraft No. 10

John Young and Michael Collins flew this spacecraft from 18 to 21 July 1966. The
[Gemini X mission report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19750067644) (MSC-G-R-66-7,
August 1966) describes it as a development of [spacecraft 9](../gt-9a/spacecraft.md).
See [the common spacecraft drawing and dimensions](../../vehicles/gemini-spacecraft.md)
for the structure and [the Agena reference](../../vehicles/agena-target-vehicle.md)
for GATV-5005, the active docking target. Collins visited the unpowered GATV-5003
from Gemini VIII separately.

Section 3.3 documents spacecraft weights of 8,295 lb at launch, 5,578 lb at
retrograde, 4,764 lb at 0.05 g reentry, 4,365 lb at main-parachute deployment and
4,254 lb at touchdown without the parachute.

## The second target

The [standalone exhibit](model/agena-8.py) shows GATV-5003 as Collins encountered
it, with the nozzle exit on the floor and the stage axis through the origin.
The closed S010 pack has four exposed outer sample panels. Its removable
fairing and the discharge fingers are still in place. Collins removed the
fairing and recovered the pack. The fingers and their attaching ring came loose
during the activity. The Gemini X report records these details in §§5.4, 7.1.2 and 8.11 and
Figs. 8.11-1 and 8.11-2.

The report identifies the four exposed panels, but gives no collector
dimensions. Its cover profiles, plates and screw heads are estimated from the
diagram and photograph. The exterior uses the Gemini VIII target's reference
finish. It does not attempt to reproduce the four months of exposure or marks
from the retrieval. The crew could not confirm whether the target's running
lights were lit, and the exhibit leaves them unlit.

## Finish and evidence limits

- Dark ribbed cabin shingles and the light adapter skin are visible together in
  Fig. 8.12-1. NASA's recovery frames S66-42782 and S66-42790 show the returned
  module, with the hatch open and the flotation collar obscuring the base. The
  cockpit's exact unflown finish, lettering dimensions and symmetry of the
  flag and UNITED STATES layout are assumed from the common Gemini drawing
  and earlier flights. [NASA Gemini X gallery](https://www.nasa.gov/gallery/gemini-x/)
- Gold-plated inner adapter surfaces are documented for spacecraft 9 through 12
  in McDonnell SEDR 300 vol. 2, p. 2-17. The surviving reentry module preserves
  postflight surfaces and excludes the discarded adapter. [Smithsonian A19680272000](https://airandspace.si.edu/collection-objects/capsule-gemini-x/nasm_A19680272000)
- The report gives the tank diameter and names its supporting structure.
  Exact tank brackets, plumbing, connector locations, door edges and paint
  details beyond photographed surfaces remain estimates. The panel and EVA
  stowage details require the spacecraft-specific drawings cited in the report
  for a full component-by-component reconstruction.
