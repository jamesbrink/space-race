# GATV-5004

The Atlas and Agena were lost together on 17 May 1966. A replacement target reached orbit on 1 June, giving Stafford and Cernan a second chance to fly the rendezvous.

Atlas SLV-3 5303 launched this uncrewed target from Cape Kennedy Launch Complex 14 on 1966-05-17 at 15:15:03.422 UTC. It carried docking adapter 5. The gallery shows the target separately at scale. The launcher and its dated pad are still absent. The exterior is shown with the launch shroud removed to expose the docking equipment. This is an exhibit configuration for the lost vehicle.

Sources include NASA SP-4002, Appendix I, Table B, and NASA CR-1106, pp. 334–336. See the [hardware notes](../../vehicles/agena-target-vehicle.md) for the geometry and its confidence.
